Classification practice & agreement studies

Classification Trainer

Classify real attack-path steps into one of the ten clusters. Practice gives instant feedback with the original analysis and the canonical wording. A Study gives several analysts the same fixed set without feedback; Results pools their rating sheets and measures how reproducibly they classify.

Scenarios are drawn evenly across the ten clusters, so rare clusters come up as often as common ones.

Start a study

Everyone in a study enters the same study code and size and gets the same scenarios in the same order. There is no feedback until the end. When you finish, download your rating sheet and send it to whoever runs the study; they load all sheets under Results.

Pool rating sheets

Load the tlctc-rating-sheet files of one study (two or more raters for inter-rater agreement).
