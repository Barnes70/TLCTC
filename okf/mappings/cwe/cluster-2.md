No physical accessibility and no physical-layer property is exploited by the attacker, so #8's generic vulnerability ("physical accessibility of infrastructure and the exploitability of physical-layer properties") does not apply — the prior mapping classified the substrate (the flaw lives in silicon) rather than the cause. Per R-ROLE (v2.4), roles are established by call direction at any interface, including intra-system privilege interfaces; the component servicing the attacker's instruction stream (CPU, kernel, hypervisor, or sandbox host) is in server-role, so the exploited generic vulnerability is a server-side implementation flaw enabling unintended behaviour (#2). Reclassified from #8 in the 2026-08-06 substrate sweep; see audit_history on the open hardware-substrate question. |
