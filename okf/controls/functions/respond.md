# RESPOND

Contain and eradicate the realized step before the next step or before a Data Risk Event matures into a Business Risk Event. Straddles the central event with DETECT, then acts on the consequence side.

# Schema

- **CSF function:** RESPOND (RS)
- **Bow-tie position:** central event → consequence side (contains)

Applies across all ten clusters; see each [control-objective set](/controls/index.md).
