# Loss of Integrity — Incorrect State (LoIi)

A refinement of Loss of Integrity where **correspondence or completeness** fails: the record no longer corresponds to what it represents, or is no longer complete. Read off the record: the content is wrong. Examples: a wiped or garbled field, a corrupted database, a value keyed wrongly on purpose by an insider, a transcript showing a command that never ran. DRE notation: `Ii` (refined) or `I` (general). Answered by validation, reconciliation and four-eyes controls. Not to be confused with a Misattributed State (`If`), where the content may be perfectly accurate but its provenance is false. The two are told apart by inspecting the record, never by who caused it.
