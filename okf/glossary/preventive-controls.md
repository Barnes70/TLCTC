# Preventive Controls

In the Bow-Tie model: barriers on the left (cause) side that reduce likelihood of threats reaching the central event. Corresponds to NIST CSF functions: IDENTIFY, PROTECT.

**Reference:** §6.1 (Bow-Tie Structure)
