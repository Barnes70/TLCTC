# Loss of Integrity — Misattributed State (LoIf)

A refinement of Loss of Integrity where **provenance or attribution** fails: the record claims an origin, author or authority it does not have, while its content may be perfectly accurate. Examples: an entry posted under someone else's open session, a forged log line, a document carrying a signature or timestamp it was not given, a tool-call transcript written by attacker code in the harness's name. DRE notation: `If` (refined) or `I` (general). Answered by segregation of duties, provenance logging and behavioural monitoring. This is the joint Parker's *Authenticity* names from the other side; TLCTC derives it from the stopping rule rather than adding a category. Not to be confused with an Incorrect State (`Ii`).
